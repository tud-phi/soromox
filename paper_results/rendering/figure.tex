    \caption{\textbf{Rendering backends and visual presets in SoRoMoX.}
    (A) The four core renderers illustrate a static equilibrium of the hanging
    soft tentacle under gravity and tendon tensions of $[6.15,0]$\,N:
    OpenCV Planar provides planar drawing, Matplotlib displays the backbone with
    coordinate axes, and Viser and Open3D render shaded surfaces in a neutral
    studio scene. OpenCV displays the equilibrium's planar projection;
    Matplotlib uses an oblique view for axis readability.
    (B) Six configurable presets illustrated with Open3D, with fixed geometry,
    pose, camera, and framing. Technical is the default preset. The presets are
    also available in Viser, with differences in lighting and shading between
    backends. The equilibrium is computed with the fitted GVS model used in the
    system-identification study; both tendons terminate at the first segment,
    leaving the second segment passive.}
