mechanics, model-based control, learning, and differentiable simulation.

## Acknowledgments

This work benefits from the broader soft-robotics and continuum-mechanics
communities.

### Theoretical Foundations

We gratefully acknowledge the foundational contributions of Prof. Federico
Renda, Prof. Frédéric Boyer, and Dr. Anup Teejo Mathew, along with the
researchers who developed the Piecewise Constant-Strain, Geometric
Variable-Strain, and related approaches implemented in this library.

### Inspiration

SoRoMoX was inspired by **SoRoSim**, a MATLAB toolbox for hybrid rigid-soft
robots:

Mathew, A. T., Hmida, I. B., Armanini, C., Boyer, F., & Renda, F. (2022).
Sorosim: A matlab toolbox for hybrid rigid–soft robots based on the geometric
variable-strain approach. *IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine*, 30(3),
106–122.
